        Glossary
        
    
          
    
            
    CA (Certificate Authority)
    An organization that signs certificates after validating who controls a name.
    
            
    Root and intermediate CA
    The root's certificate ships in the browser or operating system and its key is kept offline. Intermediates, signed by the root, do the day-to-day signing.
    
            
    CSR (Certificate Signing Request)
    The name and public key a server wants certified, signed with its own private key.
    
            
    SAN (Subject Alternative Name)
    The certificate field listing every hostname the certificate is valid for. Browsers match against this, not the Common Name.
    
            
    SNI (Server Name Indication)
    The hostname the client sends in ClientHello so a server hosting many sites can pick the right certificate.
    
            
    CRL and OCSP
    Two ways to learn a certificate was revoked: a downloadable list of revoked serial numbers, or a per-certificate status query.
    
            
    ACME
    The protocol that automates domain validation and certificate issuance and renewal.
    
            
    Forward secrecy
    Session keys come from a one-time key exchange, so a private key stolen later cannot decrypt recorded traffic.
